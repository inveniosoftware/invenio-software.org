InvenioRDM installations

Research data repositories built with InvenioRDM for managing and sharing research outputs.

Zenodo

Zenodo is a general-purpose open-access repository for the long-tail of science, enabling researchers to share and preserve any research outputs.

CERN CH 2022
InvenioRDM

Bibliothèque Virtuelle de l'Enseignement Supérieur et de la Recherche Scientifique de Côte d'Ivoire

CaltechAUTHORS

CaltechAUTHORS contains research publications by Caltech faculty and other Caltech researchers. Most items in CaltechAUTHORS are articles, but there are also books, book chapters, conference papers and more. The repository is updated continuously through library and campus efforts.

California Institute of Technology US 2014
InvenioRDM

CaltechDATA

CaltechDATA is an institutional research data repository that shares and preserves the accomplishments of Caltech researchers.

California Institute of Technology US 2019
InvenioRDM

CERN Document Server

CERN institutional repository for publications, articles, reports, multimedia content and more.

CERN CH 2023
InvenioRDM

EKP Publications

Institut für Experimentelle Teilchenphysik (ETP) at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) is an open-access repository for the scientific output of the institute.

DE
InvenioRDM

Himalayan Document Centre (HIMALDOC)

The Himalayan Document Centre (HIMALDOC) of the International Centre for Integrated Mountain Development (ICIMOD) aims to provide resources focused on sustainable mountain development (SMD) issues in various electronic formats. The resources are of several types, and include books, articles, periodicals, theses, multimedia products, and other reference materials include ICIMOD publications.

InvenioRDM

KTH Data Repository

The KTH data repository contains information about KTH research data and related research output.

KTH Royal Institute of Technology SE 2023
InvenioRDM

Materials Cloud Archive

The Materials Cloud Archive is an open and moderated repository for research data relevant to computational materials science. It aims to help replicate the results discussed in scientific publications and thereby provide added value to datasets.

EPFL CH 2020
InvenioRDM

MSD-LIVE

MSD-LIVE (MultiSector Dynamics - Living, Intuitive, Value-adding, Environment): data and code management system combined with an advanced computing platform that enables MSD researchers to document and archive their data, run their models and analysis tools, and share their data, software, and multi-model workflows.

US
InvenioRDM

Open Access Repository

Open Access Repository is an initiative supported by the DCH-RP (Digital Cultural Heritage - Roadmap for Preservation) project as it can fulfil the needs of cultural institutions that want to preserve their data and make them accessible in an Open Access way by means of a digital repository that is compliant with OpenAIRE guidelines.

IT
InvenioRDM

Prism

Prism is an institutional repository for the research and scholarly output of Northwestern University. Prism preserves intellectual works created by the scholarly community, enhances the visibility of scholarship, and promotes authors by enabling discovery and accessibility of works by the international scientific community.

Northwestern University US 2023
InvenioRDM

TU Graz Repository

TU Graz Repository is the institutional research data repository for TU Graz, the technical university of Graz, Austria. It has built-in support for several data models alongside the default.

TU Graz AT 2021
InvenioRDM

TU Wien Research Data

TU Wien Research Data is the institutional research data repository for TU Wien, the technical university of Vienna, Austria. Its is integrated with other services of TU Wien and focuses on curated datasets.

TU Wien AT 2022

Invenio v3 Framework installations

Applications and repositories built with the Invenio v3 Framework.

B2SHARE

B2SHARE is a user-friendly, reliable and trustworthy way for researchers, scientific communities and citizen scientists to store and share small-scale research data from diverse contexts.

EUDAT FI 2015
Invenio v3

CERN Open Data

Discover open Research datasets and software of LHC experiments. Visualise events and run and your own analysis.

CERN CH 2014
Invenio v3

HEPData

The Durham High Energy Physics Database (HEPData) has been built up over the past four decades as a unique open-access repository for scattering data from experimental particle physics. It currently comprises the data points from plots and tables related to several thousand publications including those from the Large Hadron Collider (LHC).

Durham University UK 2016
Invenio v3

INSPIRE-HEP

Run by a collaboration of CERN, DESY, Fermilab, IHEP, and SLAC, and interacts closely with HEP publishers, arXiv.org, NASA-ADS, PDG, HEPDATA and others.

CERN, DESY, Fermilab, SLAC CH 2017
Invenio v3

Museum Bollandianum

For almost four centuries the Bollandists have been at the forefront of hagiographic research. The Museum Bollandianum provides access to books (including the rare books collection), journals and articles of interest in that field.
